Our Story

New Company. Old Problem. Honest Beginning.

Qorino was founded in October 2026 by a small team who kept seeing the same gap: energy projects in Bangladesh and around the world are built with world-class hardware, but managed with spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and guesswork.

We are not an EPC contractor. We do not yet have a portfolio of completed power plants to show you. We have no signed collaborations as of today. What we do have is a focused capability: building the software, the management systems and the operating discipline that energy assets need to actually perform.

Our work spans solar, wind, nuclear, hydro and electric vehicles — because the energy transition is not one technology. It is a system, and someone has to manage the system.

Our Dream

To become the digital operating backbone of energy in Bangladesh and emerging markets — so that a 5 MW rooftop plant, a 200 MW solar park and a national grid operator can all run on the same intelligent, locally-built, affordable platform.

Our Target (Next 3 Years)

Deliver our first 3 pilot projects · sign 5 long-term operations contracts · launch our energy management platform in Bangla and English · build a 15-person team of engineers and software developers.
